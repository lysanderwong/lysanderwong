bugbyte

bugbyte is a creative collective run by Isolda Milenkovic and Lysander Wong. They host workshops, screenings, and other events in London.

You can keep up with their upcoming events on their instagram @bugbyte.png


bugbyte & friends - ongoing

A series of screenings to celebrate our favourite short films from around the world, with a focus on uplifting independent and emerging voices. Each edition also features a guest curator, a fellow filmmaker, who selects a few films to join the programme.
